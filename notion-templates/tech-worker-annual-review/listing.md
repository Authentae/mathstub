```markdown
**For tech workers making $200k–$700k who are tired of feeling like they're
leaving money on the table every December.**

## The promise

A 90-minute year-end review that surfaces the 2–3 things you'd otherwise miss
for another 12 months. Worked example in §0 — Priya, $310k staff PM in NY,
caught $11,300 of recoverable cash + $77k of new tax-sheltered space in

## What's inside

- **§0 Priya's worked example** — every find, every $-saved, broken down.
- **§4 Withholding strategy by income tier.** Exact W-4 line 4(c) per
  $10k of supplemental income for every gross-income tier and filing status.
- **§5 Equity portfolio concentration policy.** Risk-band table, target
  ceilings, tactics for >$1M concentrated positions (incl. exchange funds).
- **§7 AMT credit recovery multi-year planner.** Turn this year's AMT into
  next year's refund. Most people forget; it's typically 5-figure cash.
- **§8 Decision tree: ISO vs. early-exercise + 83(b).** The threshold math
  with a printable flowchart.
- **§9–§12 Cashflow, debt, insurance, estate.** The non-tax sections that
  round out a real annual review.
- **§14 Year-over-year scoreboard.** 3-column "did I move forward" table.
- **§15 24-question CPA + financial-planner dual-script.** Both pros'
  angles, written so each can answer in 30 sec.

## Who this is for

- **Yes:** $200k–$700k earners (incl. couples), W-2 + equity-heavy compensation,
  multi-state exposure, MFJ with kids or planning to have, Mega-Backdoor Roth
  available in plan.
- **No:** Solo founders with no W-2 (different planning shape), retirees,
  W-2-only people under $150k (the Tax Playbook covers your needs).

## What this is NOT

- Not financial advice.
- Not a substitute for a fiduciary CFP / CPA for actual decisions involving
  large dollar amounts. It IS the document that makes those conversations
  10× more productive.
```
