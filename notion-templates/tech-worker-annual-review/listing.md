## Long description

```markdown
**A year-end financial review for people whose finances don't look like a textbook.**

Most personal-finance templates assume a steady salary, a small portfolio, and predictable taxes. Tech worker finances do not work like that. Big chunks of comp arrive lumpy via RSU vests. Half your portfolio is in your employer's stock by year 3. The IRS quietly hits you with AMT in the year you exercise ISOs. Mega-Backdoor Roth is a thing you should be doing but no one explained.

This template is the year-end review I run on my own finances. Walk it once a year between Thanksgiving and New Year's Day.

## What you get

A single Notion page with:

- **Net worth tracker** — separates liquid, invested-retirement, employer-stock-concentrated, and unvested-future-income.
- **Income breakdown** — base, bonus, RSU at vest FMV, ESPP discount, dividends, LTCG, side-hustle, with year-over-year comparison.
- **Tax efficiency review** — effective rate, marginal rate, AMT triggered, AMT credit carryforward.
- **Equity portfolio concentration** — flag if employer-stock % is over your target ceiling, with three concrete diversification tactics.
- **Retirement contribution check** — 401(k), Mega-Backdoor Roth, Roth IRA (with backdoor if needed), HSA, 529. Limits noted for 2025; updates yearly.
- **Cash flow + savings rate** — the only two numbers that matter for FI math.
- **Debt review**, **insurance audit**, **goals tracker** — all in one place.
- **Year-over-year scoreboard** — 8 KPIs tracked across 3+ years.

## Who this is for

- Tech workers 1–10 years into a career, with RSU/ESPP/ISO grants and growing complexity.
- Anyone who has ever Googled "what is Mega-Backdoor Roth" and given up halfway.
- Couples coordinating two equity-heavy incomes.

## Who this is NOT for

- People wanting a budget app. This is annual review, not monthly tracking. Use YNAB / Monarch / Copilot for monthly.
- People wanting active tax software. The companion templates ([Year-End Tax Checklist](https://gumroad.com/) + [Equity Comp Tracker](https://gumroad.com/)) handle the "do these by Dec 31" deadlines; this template handles the "what's the trajectory" view.
```
