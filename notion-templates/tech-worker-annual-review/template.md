> 👩‍💻 **Priya, 35, staff PM at a public big-tech in NYC. $310k base + $80k bonus + $180k RSU vested + $40k Mega-Backdoor Roth available + $24k AMT credit from a 2023 ISO exercise. MFJ, husband freelances ($60k Schedule C). Two kids in elementary school.**
>
> Last year's annual review caught:
>
> | Finding | $ value |
> |---|---|
> | Forgot $8k of Mega-Backdoor Roth space (plan allowed; she didn't know) | $8,000 of tax-free future growth |

> | Husband's Solo 401(k) eligible — never opened | $69,000/yr of new sheltered space |

## 📊 §4 — Withholding strategy by income tier

Form W-4 line 4(c) is the cleanest fix for under-withholding because IRC §6654(g) deems it paid evenly across all four quarters — no penalty even if you bump it mid-year.

| Filing status | Gross income | Real marginal rate | RSU/bonus withheld at | Box 4(c) per $10k of supplemental income |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single | $50k–$95k | 22% | 22% | $0 (matches) |
| Single | $95k–$200k | 24% | 22% | $200 |
| Single | $200k–$250k | 32% | 22% | $1,000 |
| Single | $250k–$626k | 35% | 22% | $1,300 |
| Single | > $626k | 37% | 22% (or 37% > $1M YTD) | $1,500 (or $0 if 37% applied) |
| MFJ | $200k–$400k | 24% | 22% | $200 |
| MFJ | $400k–$500k | 32% | 22% | $1,000 |
| MFJ | $500k–$751k | 35% | 22% | $1,300 |
| MFJ | > $751k | 37% | 22% (or 37% > $1M YTD) | $1,500 (or $0 if 37%) |

> 💡 Add 8–13% state withholding on top in CA, NY, OR, MN, HI, NJ if employer's supplemental state rate is below the top marginal — see [50-state lookup](https://mathstub.com/state-stock-comp).

> 🔗 **For the exact box 4(c) number, run [RSU Shortfall](https://mathstub.com/rsu-tax-shortfall)** with your YTD wages + scheduled remaining vests. Divide by pay periods left.

## 🎯 §5 — Equity portfolio concentration

Tech workers concentrate in employer stock by default. The IPO/secondary that "made it" is the same one that can wipe out a decade of savings.

| Question | This year |
|---|---|
| Vested employer stock value | $— |
| Total invested net worth (excl. cash, home) | $— |
| % in employer stock | —% |
| Target ceiling | 10–15% conservative · 20% aggressive |
| Action plan | sell each vest until under ceiling, then annual harvest |

**Tactics**
- **Sell-to-cover at vest** if your broker supports it (auto-reduces accumulation).
- **Annual LTCG harvest** up to the 0% LTCG bracket if AGI is unusually low.
- **10b5-1 plan** if executive/founder/insider — set a pre-scheduled sale to legally divest during blackouts.
- **Exchange fund** if concentration > $1M and held > 7 years; trades concentration for index exposure without realizing gains (Eaton Vance, Cache, Long Angle).
- **Don't try to time IPO/secondary.** Data is bad. Decide a target allocation and trim mechanically.

> ⚠️ **The Bay Area survivor-bias graveyard:** Cisco employees who didn't diversify in 1999 lost 89% peak-to-trough. Enron, WorldCom, FTX, Bear, Lehman, SVB — same story. Conviction in your employer is not an investment thesis. It's payroll.

## 🔋 §7 — AMT credit recovery multi-year planner

When you exercised + held ISOs, the bargain element became an AMT preference, you paid AMT, and that generated a credit on Form 8801. Every future year where **regular tax > TMT** you can claim part of the credit back.

| Year | Reg tax | TMT | Credit available | Claimed | Carryforward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 (AMT year) | $40,000 | $58,000 | $0 (generated $18,000 credit) | $0 | $18,000 |
| 2025 | $52,000 | $44,000 | $18,000 | $8,000 (= reg − TMT) | $10,000 |
| 2026 | $61,000 | $48,000 | $10,000 | $10,000 | $0 ✓ |

> 🔗 Run [AMT Credit Recovery →](https://mathstub.com/amt-credit) to model this on your numbers. The credit doesn't expire; it just sits there earning $0 until you claim it.

> 💡 **The recovery accelerates when:** you have a no-RSU year (less AMTI), your ISO ages out of qualifying disposition and the lot is sold (reverses the preference), or you move to a state without state AMT.

## 🌳 §8 — Decision tree: ISO vs. early-exercise + 83(b)

```
Joining a startup, getting an ISO grant?
├── FMV ≈ strike right now (pre-Series-A, 409A is low)?
│   ├── Yes → consider early-exercise + 83(b) election within 30 days of grant
│   │       Benefit: starts LTCG clock, locks $0 bargain element forever
│   │       Risk: company fails → you spent the strike + lose it
│   └── No  → don't early-exercise; exercise as it vests, monitor AMT each year
├── Series B+ or 409A meaningfully > strike?
│   ├── Bargain element fits inside AMT exemption + your cashflow comfort?
│   │   ├── Yes → exercise + hold; pay AMT; track Form 8801 credit
│   │   └── No  → exercise + sell same-day (loses LTCG treatment, no AMT exposure)
└── About to leave the company?
    ├── Exercise window typically 90 days post-separation
    └── If exercising → run AMT projection BEFORE the trigger date
```

> ⚠️ **The "exercise + hold + IPO craters" disaster:** you owe AMT cash on FMV-at-exercise even if the stock is now worth less. The AMT credit eventually claws this back, but the cash hit is real.

> 💡 **If unsure: don't exercise + hold until you've modeled it in [ISO/AMT calc](https://mathstub.com/iso-amt).**

## 🛡️ §11 — Insurance audit

- [ ] **Term life:** if anyone depends on your income — 10–15× annual income, 20–30 yr term.
- [ ] **Disability (long-term):** employer LTD often caps at $5–10k/mo. Add private if income > coverage cap (usually $200k+ earners).
- [ ] **Umbrella liability:** cheap (~$200/yr for $1M coverage). Worth it at home or NW > $500k.
- [ ] **Auto + home:** raise deductibles to lower premiums; you have the cash buffer.
- [ ] **Health:** confirm 2026 plan elections during open enrollment. HSA-eligible if HDHP.
- [ ] **Pet (if applicable):** the math rarely works unless you'd pay $5k+ for end-of-life care.

## 📜 §12 — Estate & legal hygiene

- [ ] Will + healthcare directive in place (NoLo, Cake, or attorney)

- [ ] HSA beneficiary set (spouse = stays HSA; non-spouse = full taxable income at your death — plan accordingly)

- [ ] Power of attorney for finances + healthcare
- [ ] Document password vault + recovery plan accessible to spouse / executor
- [ ] If > $13M est. NW (single) / $26M (MFJ) in 2025: estate-tax planning conversation

## 💼 §15 — Talk-to-your-CPA + financial planner script

**For the CPA (tax angle, 12 Qs)**
1. Given YTD numbers + projected remaining vests, am I on §6654 safe harbor?
2. Did the broker report $0 cost basis on 1099-B for RSU sales? Did we adjust Form 8949 column (g)?
3. ESPP qualifying-disposition split correctly applied?
4. ISO bargain element flowed to Form 6251?
5. AMT credit on Form 8801 — claimed if regular > TMT this year?
6. Multi-state apportionment if I moved?
7. Mega-Backdoor Roth done correctly in my plan?
8. Backdoor Roth filed via Form 8606?
9. Spouse Schedule C — is Solo 401(k) better than SEP-IRA given our numbers?
10. NIIT on investment income — what triggered it; can we offset?
11. Form 4868 extension considered?
12. Any state-deduction items missed (529, charitable to a state-funded body)?

**For the financial planner (strategy angle, 12 Qs)**
13. Should I be 10b5-1'ing my employer stock?
14. Concentration policy — current %, target %, sell schedule?
15. AMT credit recovery — do we have a multi-year recovery plan?
16. Mega-Backdoor Roth space — fully utilizing each year?
17. Asset location — bonds in retirement, equities in taxable?
18. Direct indexing or tax-loss harvesting service worthwhile at my NW?
19. Roth conversion ladders during low-income years?
20. Exchange fund or DAF if very concentrated?
21. 529 over-funding strategy (can be re-purposed to Roth IRA up to $35k lifetime per SECURE 2.0)?
22. Insurance gaps?
23. State-residency change ROI?
24. Estate plan — when do we need a trust?
