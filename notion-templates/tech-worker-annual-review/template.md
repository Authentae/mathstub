# Tech Worker Annual Financial Review

> A 90-minute year-end review designed for the kind of finances tech workers actually have: lumpy RSU income, illiquid equity, single-stock concentration, and 401(k)/HSA/Mega-Backdoor moves that need planning. Pairs with the [Mathstub](https://mathstub.vercel.app) calculator suite.

---

## How to use this template

1. **Duplicate this page** into your own Notion workspace.
2. Fill in **§1 Net worth** and **§2 Income breakdown** with this year's numbers. Past years go in the **Year-over-year** table to track trajectory.
3. Walk through §3–§9 once a year, ideally between Thanksgiving and New Year's Day.
4. Each section ends with a **next-year action list** — copy those into your task manager when done.

> 💡 The goal is not perfection. The goal is to catch the 2–3 things you'd otherwise miss for another 12 months.

## §4 — Equity portfolio concentration

Tech workers concentrate in employer stock by default. The IPO/secondary that "made it" is the same one that can wipe out years of savings.

| Question | This year |
|---|---|
| Vested employer stock value | $— |
| Total invested net worth (excl. cash) | $— |
| % of portfolio in employer stock | —% |
| Target ceiling | typically 10–15% |
| Action if over ceiling | sell on each vest until under, then sell to LTCG threshold annually |

**Tactics**
- **Sell-to-cover at vest:** if your broker offers it, RSUs can sell-to-cover withholding shortfall — reduces accumulation.
- **Annual LTCG harvest:** once a year, sell employer stock up to the 0% LTCG bracket if you're under the threshold.
- **10b5-1 plan:** if you're an executive/founder with insider knowledge, set a pre-scheduled sale plan to legally divest.
- **Don't try to time IPO.** The data on this is bad. Decide a target % allocation and trim mechanically.

## §8 — Insurance audit

- [ ] **Term life:** if anyone depends on your income — generally 10–15× annual income, 20–30 yr term.
- [ ] **Disability (long-term):** employer LTD often caps at $5–10k/mo. Add private if income > coverage cap.
- [ ] **Umbrella liability:** cheap (~$200/yr for $1M). Worth it if home or net worth > $500k.
- [ ] **Auto + home:** raise deductibles to lower premiums; you have the cash buffer.
- [ ] **Health:** confirm 2026 plan elections during open enrollment. Check HSA-eligibility.
