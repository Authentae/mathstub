# Year-End Tax Checklist + Calculator Workbook

> Six months of tax moves spread across October, November, December, January, and "by April 15." Pairs with the [Mathstub](https://mathstub.com) calculator suite.

---

## How to use this template

1. **Duplicate this page** into your own Notion workspace.
2. Each month-section is a checklist. Walk it the first weekend of that month — most items take under 30 minutes.
3. The **Calculator workbook** at the bottom maps every item to the right Mathstub tool. Don't think; just click.
4. The **Tax-doc inventory** is your "did I receive everything?" sweep before you file.

> 💡 Most year-end tax mistakes happen because the right move had a deadline that was 6 weeks earlier than the user thought. This template's job is to make those deadlines visible.

---

## 🍂 October checklist

The most leverage month of the year. There's still time to fix everything.

- [ ] Re-run the [Quarterly Estimated Tax Safe-Harbor calculator](https://mathstub.com/quarterly-estimated-tax) with full-year actuals projected. Confirm withholding + estimates will hit the §6654 target by Jan 15.
- [ ] If short, choose: (a) update Form W-4 line 4(c) for extra per-paycheck withholding, OR (b) plan a Jan 15 estimated payment.
- [ ] Re-run the [RSU shortfall calculator](https://mathstub.com/rsu-tax-shortfall) for any Q4 vest already scheduled. Withholding gap = your action item.
- [ ] Confirm Q3 estimated tax payment was credited (login to IRS Direct Pay or your accountant's portal).
- [ ] Review held ISOs against year-end AMT exposure ([ISO/AMT calculator](https://mathstub.com/iso-amt)). If huge, decide whether to disqualify-sell some lots before Dec 31 to manage cash.
- [ ] Confirm 401(k) is on track to max ($23,500 + $7,500 catch-up if 50+ in 2025; verify 2026 limits at IRS).
- [ ] Confirm HSA is on track to max ($4,300 individual / $8,550 family in 2025; check 2026 figure).
- [ ] If self-employed: max Solo 401(k) and SEP-IRA contributions before year-end.

## 🦃 November checklist

The "decide-and-act" month. Most year-end strategies have a Dec 1 cliff.

- [ ] **Tax-loss harvesting:** review brokerage holdings. Sell underwater lots to offset realized gains. Watch the wash-sale rule — wait 30 days before re-buying the same security.
- [ ] **Charitable giving plan:** decide bunch-and-itemize vs. take standard deduction. Most tech workers in 2025/26 take standard ($15,000 single / $30,000 MFJ).
- [ ] If itemizing: schedule donations for delivery before Dec 31. DAF (Donor-Advised Fund) contributions count in the year deposited.
- [ ] **FSA (medical) spend-down:** healthcare-FSA money expires Dec 31 (some plans allow $640 carryover or 2.5-month grace; check your plan). Schedule appointments now.
- [ ] **Dependent-care FSA:** ensure you'll use the full balance before Mar 15 grace period.
- [ ] If you'll have a big year (RSUs vested + bonus + sale): consider a **Roth conversion** in December ONLY if next year's marginal rate will be lower. Run the [RSU shortfall calc](https://mathstub.com/rsu-tax-shortfall) first to confirm no AMT trip.
- [ ] **Open enrollment housekeeping:** confirm 2026 HSA / FSA / 401(k) elections are correct for January.

## 🎄 December checklist

The "last-chance" month.

- [ ] Confirm 401(k) will max out in the December paychecks. If you under-contributed, increase the deferral % for the last 1-2 paychecks.
- [ ] Make any remaining charitable contributions by Dec 31 (gifts must be **delivered** by Dec 31, not mailed Jan 2).
- [ ] If you owe Q4 estimated tax: schedule for Jan 15 next year. Or pre-pay before Dec 31 if state needs Q4 paid in calendar year.
- [ ] Sell any remaining tax-loss-harvest lots before Dec 31 to count the loss this year.
- [ ] **ESPP last-purchase-date check:** if your purchase happened in 2024-12 (24+ months ago) and the previous purchase was 2024-06 (now > 24 months from offer date), some shares may now qualify. Plan an early-2026 sale.
- [ ] **DAF / charitable-stock contributions:** appreciated stock goes further than cash for charity. Last day to fund: Dec 31. Most DAF providers need the transfer initiated by mid-December.
- [ ] Decide on a 529 contribution for kids' education before Dec 31 if your state offers a state-tax deduction.

---

## ❄️ January checklist

The "incoming docs" month.

- [ ] Send the **Q4 estimated tax payment** by Jan 15 (federal + any state quarterly).
- [ ] Watch for W-2 (Jan 31 deadline; usually arrives mid-month via payroll portal).
- [ ] Watch for 1099-INT (banks), 1099-DIV (brokerages), 1099-B (sales).
- [ ] Watch for 1099-R if you took a distribution.
- [ ] Watch for **Form 3922** (ESPP purchase reporting) and **Form 3921** (ISO exercise reporting). Required if you exercised or purchased.
- [ ] Watch for K-1s if you have private partnership investments (often delayed to March).
- [ ] If you contracted: 1099-NEC ($600+ from any single payer).
- [ ] Set up a 2026 file folder for tax docs.

---

## 🌷 February checklist

The "verify-and-prepare" month.

- [ ] Reconcile each W-2 box against your final paystub (Box 1 = federal taxable wages — confirm RSU vest income flowed through).
- [ ] Reconcile 1099-B totals against your **Sales log** in the [Equity Comp Tracker](https://mathstub.com) template. Common discrepancy: your broker reports the *whole* sale price as cost basis on RSU sales, but your real basis includes the FMV at vest already taxed as wages. The IRS will overcharge you if you don't fix this manually on Form 8949.
- [ ] Re-check ESPP qualifying-disposition status for any lots sold in January using the [ESPP calculator](https://mathstub.com/espp-qualifying-disposition).
- [ ] If you exercised ISOs in 2025 and held: confirm Form 3921 received. The bargain element is your AMT preference for Form 6251.
- [ ] Schedule a tax-software session OR your CPA appointment.

---

## 🌸 March / April checklist

The "file-or-extend" stretch.

- [ ] If you can't gather everything by April 15: file Form 4868 for an automatic extension to Oct 15. **An extension to file is NOT an extension to pay** — you still have to send any owed amount by April 15.
- [ ] Make a **traditional IRA or Roth IRA contribution** for the prior year by April 15 (no extension on this).
- [ ] Make a **prior-year HSA contribution** by April 15 (also no extension).
- [ ] If self-employed: prior-year SEP-IRA contributions allowed up to extended return due date (Oct 15).
- [ ] After filing: copy your final return into the **Tax-doc inventory** below, scan a PDF backup, and store securely.

## 📥 Tax-doc inventory

Check off each one as it arrives. Multiple of the same type? Add a row per source.

| Form | Source | Expected by | Received? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W-2 | Employer | Jan 31 | ☐ | Box 1 = federal taxable wages |
| 1099-INT | Bank | Feb 15 | ☐ | Interest > $10 |
| 1099-DIV | Brokerage | Feb 15 | ☐ | Dividends |
| 1099-B | Brokerage | Feb 15 | ☐ | Stock sales — verify basis manually for RSU lots |
| 1099-NEC | 1099 client | Jan 31 | ☐ | $600+ from any single payer |
| 1099-MISC | Various | Jan 31 | ☐ | Royalties, rent, prizes |
| 1099-R | Retirement plan | Jan 31 | ☐ | Distributions |
| 1098 | Mortgage servicer | Jan 31 | ☐ | Mortgage interest |
| 1098-T | School | Jan 31 | ☐ | Tuition statements |
| Form 3921 | Employer | Jan 31 | ☐ | ISO exercise — keep for AMT calc |
| Form 3922 | Employer | Jan 31 | ☐ | ESPP purchase — keep for sale-year basis |
| K-1 | Partnership / S-corp / trust | Mar 15 (often slips) | ☐ | Plan an extension if expected |
| 5498 | Bank/brokerage | May 31 | ☐ | IRA contributions — INFO only, after-filing |
