## 🍂 §2 October checklist

The most leverage month of the year. There's still time to fix everything.

- [ ] **Re-run [Quarterly Estimated Tax Safe-Harbor](https://mathstub.com/quarterly-estimated-tax)** with full-year actuals projected. Confirm withholding + estimates will hit §6654 target by Jan 15.
- [ ] If short: file W-4 line 4(c) for extra per-paycheck withholding (treated as paid evenly per IRC §6654(g)), OR plan a Jan 15 estimated payment.
- [ ] **Re-run [RSU Shortfall](https://mathstub.com/rsu-tax-shortfall)** for any Q4 vest scheduled. The withholding gap = your action item.
- [ ] Confirm Q3 estimated tax payment was credited (IRS Direct Pay receipt or your accountant's portal).
- [ ] **AMT exposure check:** if you exercised + held ISOs, run [ISO/AMT calc](https://mathstub.com/iso-amt). If 5-figure exposure, decide whether to disqualify-sell some lots before Dec 31 to manage cash.
- [ ] Confirm 401(k) on track to max — verify with paycheck calculator: `(YTD deferred + Dec paychecks × deferral %) ≥ $23,500` (`$31,000` if 50+ in 2026).
- [ ] Confirm HSA on track to max ($4,300 single / $8,550 family in 2025; verify 2026 limits at IRS Pub 969 in late Q4).
- [ ] **Mega-backdoor Roth** check: does your 401(k) plan allow after-tax contributions + in-service distributions? If yes, fund the gap to the $70k overall §415 limit.
- [ ] If self-employed: max Solo 401(k) employee deferral by Dec 31; employer portion can wait until tax-return-due-date including extensions.
- [ ] **Tax-loss harvesting plan:** list current loss positions in your taxable brokerage. Earmark Dec sells. Note wash-sale risk on each.
- [ ] **State residency** check: if you moved this year, log start/end dates per state and document a single tax home.

## 🦃 §3 November checklist

The "decide-and-act" month. Most year-end strategies have a Dec 1 cliff.

- [ ] **Tax-loss harvest:** sell underwater lots to offset realized gains. **Wash-sale rule:** wait > 30 days before re-buying the same security; substantially identical funds count.
- [ ] **Charitable giving plan:** decide bunch-and-itemize vs. standard deduction. Most tech workers in 2025/26 take standard ($15,000 single / $30,000 MFJ).
- [ ] If itemizing: schedule donations for delivery by Dec 31. DAF (Donor-Advised Fund) contributions count in year deposited.
- [ ] **Appreciated-stock-to-charity check:** giving > $5k of appreciated stock avoids cap-gains tax AND deducts FMV. Most DAFs need 2–3 weeks to process — initiate by Nov 30.
- [ ] **FSA (health) spend-down:** balance expires Dec 31 (some plans allow up to $660 carryover or a 2.5-mo grace; verify your SPD). Schedule appointments now.
- [ ] **Dependent-care FSA:** confirm you'll use the full balance before Mar 15 grace.
- [ ] If big-income year (RSUs + bonus + sale): consider a **Roth conversion** in December ONLY if next year's marginal rate will be lower. Run [RSU Shortfall](https://mathstub.com/rsu-tax-shortfall) first to confirm no AMT trigger.
- [ ] **Open enrollment housekeeping:** confirm 2026 HSA / FSA / 401(k) elections for January.
- [ ] **Backdoor Roth** runway: pristine the traditional IRA (rollover to 401(k) if pro-rata rule would hurt) before December.

## 🎄 §4 December checklist

The "last-chance" month.

- [ ] Confirm 401(k) maxes in December paychecks. If under, bump deferral % for the last 1–2 paychecks.
- [ ] Make remaining **charitable cash** contributions by Dec 31 (gifts must be **delivered** by Dec 31 — not mailed Jan 2).
- [ ] Send Q4 federal estimate by Jan 15 (state may want it by Dec 31 to count in calendar year — check your state DOR).
- [ ] Sell remaining tax-loss-harvest lots — must settle by Dec 31.
- [ ] **DAF / appreciated-stock contributions:** initiate transfer by mid-December (broker timelines vary).
- [ ] **529 contributions** for state-tax deduction by Dec 31.
- [ ] **HSA contribution** through payroll for the last paycheck (Apr 15 deadline if going direct).
- [ ] **ESPP last-purchase-date check:** if your purchase happened > 24 months ago AND prior purchase > 24 months from offer date, some shares may now qualify. Plan an early-2026 sale via [ESPP calc](https://mathstub.com/espp-qualifying-disposition).
- [ ] If you turned 73 this year: take your first RMD by April 1; for years 74+, RMD due Dec 31.
- [ ] **Cap-gain harvest** at the 0% LTCG bracket if AGI is unusually low (sabbatical, between jobs, parental leave).

## ❄️ §5 January checklist

The "incoming docs + final estimate" month.

- [ ] **Q4 estimated tax payment by Jan 15** (federal + state quarterly). Use [Quarterly calc](https://mathstub.com/quarterly-estimated-tax) one last time.
- [ ] Watch for **W-2** (Jan 31 deadline; usually mid-month via payroll).
- [ ] **1099-INT** (banks), **1099-DIV / 1099-B** (brokerages — many run late, often corrected Feb–Mar).
- [ ] **1099-NEC** if you contracted ($600+ from any single payer).
- [ ] **1099-R** if you took a retirement distribution.
- [ ] **Form 3921** (ISO exercise) and **Form 3922** (ESPP purchase) — required if you exercised or purchased.
- [ ] **K-1** if you have private partnership / S-corp investments (often delayed to March).
- [ ] Set up a 2026 file folder + Notion page for tax docs.
- [ ] **Reconcile final paystub Box 1 against W-2 Box 1** — should match. Discrepancies → call payroll before they go quiet.

## 🌷 §6 February checklist

The "verify-and-prepare" month.

- [ ] **W-2 Box 1 reconciliation:** equals (gross wages − pre-tax 401(k) − pre-tax HSA − pre-tax health/dental − DCA FSA) + RSU FMV-at-vest + ESPP discount + ISO disqualifying-disposition ordinary portion. Off by > $50? Call payroll.
- [ ] **1099-B audit:** reconcile against §3 Sales log in the [Equity Comp Tracker](https://gumroad.com/) template. Broker reports $0 cost basis on RSU sales — **manually adjust on Form 8949 column (g)** or you'll be taxed twice.
- [ ] **ESPP qualifying-disposition split:** re-run [ESPP calc](https://mathstub.com/espp-qualifying-disposition) for each lot sold. Ordinary-income piece goes on line 1h of your 1040.
- [ ] **ISO exercised + held in prior year:** confirm Form 3921 received. Bargain element flows to AMTI on Form 6251.
- [ ] Schedule a tax-software session OR CPA appointment.

---

## 🌸 §7 March / April checklist

The "file-or-extend" stretch.

- [ ] If you can't gather everything by April 15 → file Form 4868 for automatic extension to Oct 15. **Extension to file ≠ extension to pay.** Send any owed amount by April 15 to avoid penalty.
- [ ] **Traditional or Roth IRA prior-year contribution by April 15** — no extension.
- [ ] **Prior-year HSA contribution** by April 15 — no extension.
- [ ] If self-employed: prior-year SEP-IRA contributions allowed up to extended due date (Oct 15).
- [ ] After filing: copy your final return into §8 Tax-doc inventory, scan a PDF backup, store securely.
- [ ] **Mark April 15 as Day 1 of next year's planning** — start §2 October work on January 2 if you had a > $5k April 15 surprise.

## ✍️ §10 W-4 line 4(c) workbook

Form W-4 box 4(c) lets you add extra federal withholding per paycheck. It's the **best fix** for a mid-year shortfall because IRC §6654(g) treats it as evenly paid across all four quarters — no penalty even if you bump it in October.

```
Shortfall to cover this year   $______ (from Mathstub RSU calc)
Pay periods left this year     ÷ _____ (paychecks remaining)
                               ─────────
Box 4(c) entry                 $______ per paycheck
```

| Scenario | Shortfall | Pay periods left | Box 4(c) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big Q3 RSU vest, found gap in October | $7,200 | 6 (bi-weekly) | $1,200 |
| Found gap in late November | $7,200 | 2 | $3,600 |
| Q4 vest in December | $4,000 | 1 | $4,000 |

> 💡 **Submit to payroll, not HR.** Tell payroll the effective pay period. Confirm on the next pay-stub that the additional amount actually came out.

> ⚠️ **Don't decrease box 4(c) the following January and forget.** It will keep withholding extra in 2027. Reset on the first January paycheck of the new year.

## 🧾 §11 Pre-filing self-audit (one hour, do it in March)

Run this before you hit "file" in TurboTax or send to your CPA.

- [ ] Box 1 of W-2 = (gross wages − pre-tax retirement − pre-tax health − pre-tax FSA + RSU FMV + ESPP discount + ISO DD)
- [ ] Form 8949 — every RSU lot has manually-adjusted basis (column g)
- [ ] Form 6251 — AMTI includes ISO bargain element for held exercises
- [ ] Form 8801 — AMT credit carryforward applied if regular tax > TMT
- [ ] Schedule D totals match Form 8949 lines
- [ ] State return matches federal numbers (don't enter state-only adjustments to the federal columns)
- [ ] If MFJ: spouse's W-2 + 1099s all included
- [ ] If you moved: each state's part-year return apportions correctly
- [ ] Direct-deposit refund or auto-pay-balance set up
- [ ] Filed Form 4868 if not ready (it's free; do it as insurance)
