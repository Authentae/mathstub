## Daniel's worked example

CA→TX move in Aug 2024, 1,600 unvested RSUs from a 2022 grant. He assumed:
"I'm a TX resident now → no state tax on anything that vests from here on."

This planner showed him the real picture: **$34,000 of CA tax still owed across
2024–2026** because of work-source allocation (CA claims the vesting months when
he physically worked there, even if he's no longer a resident). Caught in
October, fixable via quarterly estimates → **avoided $1,400 of penalties on top.**
