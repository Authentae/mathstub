## After-tweet engagement playbook

For first 3 hours post-thread:

1. **Quote-tweet your own thread with a different angle** every 30 min:
   - "$6,035 — that's the average shortfall on a $50k RSU vest in CA. Here's why."
   - "TurboTax handles filing. Carta tracks vests. Mathstub does the planning math nobody else does."

2. **Reply to people who reply** — within 5 min if you can. Even "thanks!" is fine.

3. **DM 5 specific tech-worker-influencer accounts** with a personal note + the link:
   - People you actually follow (don't spam strangers)
   - Mention what you'd want from them: "happy to add your feedback in v2"

4. **If your tweet gets a quote-tweet from a verified account** — engage with it within 10 min. That's where viral lifts come from.

## Don't

- Don't tweet "PLZ UPVOTE ON PH 🙏🙏🙏" — desperate, hurts both PH ranking + your reputation.
- Don't tweet the PH link 5 times. Once is fine; second one as a quote-tweet is OK; third+ is spam.
- Don't reply to negative tweets with sales pitch. "Sorry it's not for you" is the right reply, then move on.
- Don't auto-post to LinkedIn the same thread. LinkedIn audience is different (see LinkedIn post draft).
