## Do this for each of the 4 products

General steps (same every time):
1. Go to **gumroad.com** → log in → **Products** → click the product.
2. In the editor, find the **Cover** / thumbnail area near the top. Remove the old image, then
   drag in the new `cover.png` from the matching folder below.
3. Scroll to the product **description / content images**. Replace the old preview images by
   dragging in `trust-whats-inside.jpg` and `trust-why-trust.jpg`.
4. Click **Save changes** (top right). Open the public link to eyeball it.
