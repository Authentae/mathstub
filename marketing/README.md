# Marketing — launch posts

Three posts to drive initial traffic to mathstub.com after launch.

## Posting order

| Day | Post | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 (Tue/Wed AM ET) | **r/personalfinance — RSU shortfall** | Front page of /r/PF, ~5–15k visitors |
| Day 2 (24h later) | **r/cscareerquestions — ISO/AMT** | Different angle, hits early-career engineers |
| Day 3 (24h after that) | **Show HN** | Front page traffic, builds dev/founder credibility |

## Success metrics to watch

- **Day 1–3:** mathstub.com analytics. Look for spikes from `reddit.com/r/personalfinance` and `news.ycombinator.com` referrers.
- **Week 1:** Gumroad sales count. Even 1 sale on each template means the funnel works.
- **Week 2:** AdSense/FlexOffers approvals likely landing in this window. Once AdSense is live, every visitor compounds.
- **Day 7+:** Repeat the post cycle on different subreddits if traffic dropped:
  - r/financialindependence (post the Annual Review angle)
  - r/povertyfinance (skip — wrong audience)
  - r/tax (smaller, but quality)
  - r/Bogleheads (if you write a tax-loss-harvesting angle)
  - LinkedIn long-form (if you have any tech-worker followers)

## Don't do

- **Cross-post the same content same-day.** Reddit's spam filter eats it.
- **Reply to "is this an ad" with a sales pitch.** Reply with: "It's free, ad-supported on the rest of the site. Built it because I was tired of doing the math by hand. Happy to skip the link if it bothers you."
- **Edit the post 30 min after submission to "improve" it.** Reddit's algorithm punishes edits.
- **Put the calculator URL in the title.** Mods auto-remove. Body links only.

## After the launch

If a post hits front page, expect:
- Comments asking "does it support [edge case]?" — answer truthfully, log feature requests.
- Mods or users flagging promotional content — engage politely, point to the disclaimer + open-source plans.
- A spike of Gumroad traffic. If conversion is <0.5%, the templates aren't right for this audience. Iterate or kill.
