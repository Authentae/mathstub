# Free Notion magnet — listing copy + how to publish

> **Why this exists:** Selling *paid* Notion templates requires a months-long waitlist + Stripe (see `notion-marketplace-paid-blocked.md`). A **free** template needs neither — review is 1–3 business days. This is a new discovery channel (Notion Marketplace search) that bypasses Google's new-domain sandbox entirely. Claude built the template content (`equity-comp-tax-tracker-lite.md`); your part is the ~5-min publish below.

---

## Step 1 — Get the template into Notion (~3 min)

1. In Notion, create a new page.
2. Click **⋯ (top right) → Import → Markdown & CSV**, and import `equity-comp-tax-tracker-lite.md` from this folder. (Or just copy-paste its contents into a blank page — Notion renders the headings, tables, and checkboxes automatically.)
3. Optional polish: turn the two tables ("Grant tracker", "Vest & sale log") into proper Notion databases — hover the table, click the **⋮⋮** handle → **Turn into database**. Makes them filterable. Not required for launch.
4. Set a page icon (📊) and a simple cover. Keep it clean.

## Step 2 — Make it shareable

1. Top-right **Share** → **Publish** → toggle **Publish to web**.
2. Enable **Allow duplicate as template** (this is what lets people copy it into their own Notion).
3. Copy the public URL — you'll need it for the listing and you can paste it back here so Claude links to it from the site.

## Step 3 — Submit to Notion Marketplace (~2 min)

1. Go to **https://www.notion.com/templates** → **Submit a template** (you may need a free creator profile first).
2. Use the listing copy below.
3. Submit. Review is typically 1–3 business days. Free listings have no waitlist and no Stripe requirement.
