## 4. Year-end checklist (the 6 that go irreversible at midnight Dec 31)

- [ ] Project total tax vs what's been withheld — find the gap
- [ ] Top up **W-4 line 4(c)** before your last December paycheck (retroactively cures earlier under-withholding)
- [ ] Max 401(k) + HSA through payroll
- [ ] Tax-loss harvest (watch the 30-day wash-sale rule)
- [ ] Donate appreciated stock to charity if planned (must settle by 12/31)
- [ ] Decide on any ISO exercise timing for AMT

Full 10-item version with IRS citations: [Year-end equity comp checklist](https://www.mathstub.com/blog/year-end-equity-comp-checklist).
