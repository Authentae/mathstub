US W-2 employees with equity compensation. Mostly mobile, mostly anxious, mostly underserved by generic tax tools. They arrive after a vest, a payroll surprise, or a CPA invoice they didn’t expect.

  • Income Median household $150K–$500K
  • Employer Public & late-stage private tech, W-2 with RSU/ISO/ESPP
